EZ Cart Dev Log: Build in Public - May 08, 2026

Building EZ Cart: Our Journey, Publicly Shared

Welcome to the EZ Cart community! We're thrilled to introduce EZ Cart, your new privacy-first universal price tracker Chrome extension. But more than just introducing a product, we want to invite you behind the scenes. We believe in the power of transparency, especially when building a tool designed to protect your privacy. That's why we're committing to a "Build in Public" approach.

What Does "Build in Public" Mean for EZ Cart?

For us, building in public means sharing our progress, our challenges, and our victories with you, every step of the way. It means being open about our development roadmap, the features we're prioritizing, and even the bugs we encounter. You'll get insights into our design decisions, our technical hurdles, and how we're ensuring EZ Cart remains truly privacy-first – no sneaky tracking, ever. This isn't just about showing off; it's about fostering trust and accountability. In a world where data privacy is paramount, we believe our commitment to openness will reinforce EZ Cart's foundational promise.

Your Voice Shapes Our Future

Building in public is a two-way street. We're not just broadcasting; we're listening. Your feedback, suggestions, and even criticisms are invaluable. You are the users, and your experience is central to EZ Cart's evolution. Want a specific retailer added? Have an idea for a new feature that enhances your shopping experience while maintaining privacy? Encountered an issue? We want to hear from you! Follow our journey on X (formerly Twitter) @EZCartHQ, join our Discord server (link coming soon!), or simply drop us an email.

This isn't just an extension; it's a community project. Together, we can build the best, most private price tracker out there. Install EZ Cart today and join us on this exciting, transparent adventure!
