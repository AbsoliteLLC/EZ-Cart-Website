Your Privacy, Our Priority: EZ Cart's Secure Price Tracking

Tired of feeling watched online? Every click and purchase tracked and analyzed. While saving money is great, sacrificing personal privacy shouldn't be the cost. EZ Cart offers a privacy-first universal price tracker Chrome extension, putting you back in control.

Many price trackers collect vast amounts of your browsing data. They know what you look at, what you consider buying, and even what you abandon. This data is often used for targeted ads, sold to third parties, or stored in vulnerable databases. Your shopping habits become a commodity, growing your digital footprint and making you a target for marketing ploys and security risks.

EZ Cart takes a fundamentally different approach. We believe price tracking can be done without compromising your digital freedom. Our extension respects your privacy by keeping your data where it belongs: on your device. EZ Cart processes product information locally, without sending your browsing history, purchase intentions, or personal details to external servers. We don't build profiles, sell data, or track you across the web.

This local processing not only safeguards your privacy but also enhances your security. With no sensitive data leaving your browser for our servers, there's no data for us to lose or for malicious actors to steal. It significantly reduces your exposure to data breaches. EZ Cart gives you powerful price tracking, securely and privately.
