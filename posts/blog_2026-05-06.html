EZ Cart Dev Log: Tech Deep Dive - May 06, 2026

EZ Cart's Core: Your Price Tracking, Your Privacy

Ever wondered what goes on behind the scenes of a price tracker? With EZ Cart, we believe transparency, especially around your data, is paramount. Today, we're taking a tech deep dive into how our privacy-first universal price tracker Chrome extension works, ensuring your shopping habits remain your own business. It's not magic; it's meticulous engineering.

At its heart, EZ Cart operates entirely client-side. Unlike many trackers that relay your browsing data to central servers for processing, EZ Cart keeps everything local on your machine. When you visit an e-commerce page, our lightweight extension intelligently identifies product information and pricing directly from your browser. This data never leaves your device and is stored securely in your Chrome's local storage.

This architecture is fundamental to our "privacy-first" pledge. There are no user accounts, no external databases logging your activity, and absolutely no data sharing with third parties. Your price watchlists, your historical price graphs, and your saved deals are all housed within your browser instance. We don't track you across sites, build user profiles, or sell your data—because we simply don't collect it.

The security implications are profound. Without a centralized server storing your shopping history, there's no single point of attack for malicious actors to exploit your personal data. You maintain complete control and ownership over your information. This design choice represents a significant departure from conventional data collection models, prioritizing user autonomy above all else.
